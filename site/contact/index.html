Questions

Where are my workouts stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud account, which keeps your devices in sync. There is no LOGIT account to sign up for.

How do I get LOGIT Pro back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings › Subscription in LOGIT and tap Restore Purchase.

How do I cancel LOGIT Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. LOGIT's Settings › Subscription takes you there too.

Does LOGIT work with Apple Health?

Yes. Under Settings › Apple Health, finished workouts are saved to Apple Health as strength training, and body weight and body fat stay in sync both ways.

Can I keep a copy of my data?

Settings › Data › Export All Data saves every workout, exercise, template and measurement as a file you keep, readable in any text editor.
